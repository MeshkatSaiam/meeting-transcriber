Meeting Transcriber Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Meeting Transcriber is a privacy-first web application designed for verbatim meeting transcription and speaker diarization. We respect your personal data and maintain strict privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

• Account Information: When you authenticate using Google Sign-In or email, we receive your email address and basic profile name to create and manage your secure account session.

• Speaker Voice Profiles: Voice sample clips you choose to enroll are encrypted and stored in your private database partition protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS). No other user can access or view your voice samples.

• Audio Recordings & Transcripts: Audio files you transcribe are processed client-side in your browser and sent directly to Google Gemini API for transcription. Your audio files are never permanently stored on external web servers.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or distribute your personal data, voice samples, or transcripts to any third-party advertisers. All cloud synchronization is powered by Supabase with end-to-end Row-Level Security isolation.

4. Contact & Account Deletion

You may delete any enrolled voice profiles directly inside the application at any time. For questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact the maintainer at meshkatsaiam@gmail.com.
